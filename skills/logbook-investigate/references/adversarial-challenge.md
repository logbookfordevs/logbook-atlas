# Adversarial challenge

Use for a selected consequential inference whose weak assumptions could change the answer. This is one bounded challenge, not the full continuous Doubt Driven Development workflow.

Isolate the smallest reviewable evidence or proposal and its question, constraints, and acceptance standard. Give an authorized fresh-context reviewer that material without the author's persuasive rationale. Ask for material counterexamples, unsupported assumptions, and contract violations, with evidence; an empty findings list is a valid result.

Reconcile each finding against the source material. Separate an unclear contract from an actionable flaw, an accepted trade-off, or unsupported noise. Investigate a material flaw and correct the conclusion; do not change product code merely because a reviewer suggests it.

A research worker asks its caller to arrange any fresh-context review rather than spawning another worker. If independent review is unavailable or unauthorized, use available evidence checks and label the result as a self-check, never fresh-context validation. Do not claim independent verification or stall unrelated research.

Re-check only a material correction that leaves unresolved uncertainty. Stop when the targeted uncertainty is resolved or further progress requires missing evidence or a user decision; cap this bounded method at three cycles and report unresolved findings. Cross-model review is optional when requested and supported by the host, not a mandatory offer or a new approval ritual.
