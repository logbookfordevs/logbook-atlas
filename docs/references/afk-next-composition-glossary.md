# Composition glossary from AFK Next

Historical terminology introduced during this proposal, preserved from AFK’s CONTEXT.md. The current proposal refines ownership under Atlas.


**Capability Composition**:
A relationship in which one skill uses another independently available capability to perform its work.
_Avoid_: Source Composition, source inclusion

**Source Composition**:
The incorporation and reconciliation of selected source material into behavior maintained by AFK.
_Avoid_: Capability Composition, skill invocation

**Source Provenance**:
The record of the origins and adaptations of material incorporated into AFK-maintained behavior.
_Avoid_: Skill Provenance, installation origin
